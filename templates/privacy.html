Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or any financial account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filters. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app on your behalf.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app, but these providers do not have access to your expense data beyond what is necessary to run the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
